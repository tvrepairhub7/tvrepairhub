How does your repair process work, from booking to handover?

Register your complaint on this website, call us, or message us on WhatsApp. Our team then contacts you and a technician is sent to your home in Lucknow, with an average response time of 30 to 50 minutes. At your home the technician inspects the TV, explains the fault in simple language and shares the charges. Work begins only after you approve. When the repair is finished, the TV is tested and handed back to you with a 90-day warranty on labour and parts.

What are your charges and how do they work?

A visit charge of ₹249 applies for each home visit anywhere in Lucknow. If a part has to be replaced, a part replacement service charge of ₹649 applies. After the inspection, the technician shares the final quote and explains what you are being charged for, and no work starts until you say yes. If you have any question about a charge, please ask the technician or call us before you approve.

What exactly does the 90-day warranty cover?

Every repair we complete carries a 90-day warranty on both labour and parts. If a problem related to your repair appears within those 90 days, contact us on +91 93696 52109 or +91 83034 67882 and we will look into it. Keep your Complaint ID handy, or tell us the phone number used while booking, so we can find your repair record quickly.

Why do you not repair TVs that are under manufacturer warranty?

We work only on out-of-warranty TVs. A TV that is still under the brand’s warranty should be repaired by the brand’s authorised service centre, because repair by a third party may affect that warranty. Once your warranty has ended, we are happy to help with fast home service and clear pricing.

Which TVs and brands do you repair?

We repair LED, LCD, OLED, QLED, plasma and Smart TVs of all major brands, including Samsung, LG, Sony, Mi, Redmi, OnePlus, TCL, Panasonic, Philips, Vu, Hisense and many more. We handle display and panel faults, backlight, power and motherboard problems, software issues, sound and speaker problems, and HDMI and USB ports. If you are not sure what is wrong, use the Quick Diagnosis tool above or simply describe the problem in the complaint form.
